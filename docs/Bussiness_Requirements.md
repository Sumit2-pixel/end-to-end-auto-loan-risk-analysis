# Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze an auto loan portfolio to assess portfolio health, identify high-risk segments, estimate potential credit losses, and provide actionable insights that support risk monitoring and business decision-making.

# Business Problem
The bank manages a large portfolio of auto loans but lacks a clear understanding of portfolio health, high-risk segments, regional risk concentration, and potential credit losses. Without this visibility, it becomes difficult to take timely risk mitigation actions and make informed business decisions.

# Stakeholders
- Risk Management Team
- Credit Risk Analysts
- Portfolio Managers
- Senior Management / Executives
- Collections & Recovery Team
- Business Strategy Team

# Success Metrics

- Provide a clear view of overall portfolio health.
- Identify high-risk loans and segments accurately.
- Highlight regions and loan categories with higher credit risk.
- Support timely risk monitoring and data-driven business decisions.
- Enable better credit risk assessment through actionable insights.

# Expected Outcome
The analysis is expected to provide a comprehensive view of portfolio performance, improve credit risk assessment, identify high-risk segments, support proactive risk mitigation, and enable data-driven lending and portfolio management decisions.
